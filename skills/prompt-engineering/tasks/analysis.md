# Analysis

## Goal
Analyze supplied information against explicit criteria and produce a decision-useful interpretation without inventing missing evidence.

## Required inputs
- object/question to analyze
- relevant data or source scope
- evaluation criteria when applicable
- requested output form

## Rules
- Separate observations from interpretations and recommendations.
- Make comparison criteria explicit when comparing alternatives.
- Preserve uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Use calculations only when inputs and method are defined.

## Validation
- Analysis addresses the stated question.
- Conclusions follow from presented evidence.
- Material assumptions and limitations are visible.
- Requested criteria are covered consistently.

## Do not
- manufacture scores or metrics without a defined basis;
- substitute generic advice for requested analysis;
- introduce model-specific reasoning instructions here.
