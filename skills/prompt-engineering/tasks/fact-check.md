# Fact Check

## Goal
Assess a claim against available evidence and return a transparent conclusion without overstating certainty.

## Required inputs
- claim to verify
- permitted evidence scope
- relevant date/context when material

## Rules
- Break complex claims into independently checkable subclaims when needed.
- Separate factual verification from opinion or prediction.
- Prefer primary evidence when available and permitted.
- Preserve contradictory evidence and explain why it matters.
- Use a verdict vocabulary only when its criteria are defined.

## Validation
- The exact claim is addressed.
- Evidence supports the stated level of confidence.
- Material context and date boundaries are included.
- Unknown or unverified elements remain explicitly unresolved.

## Do not
- infer truth from repetition or popularity;
- fabricate citations or certainty;
- convert a fact-check into unrelated persuasion or recommendation.
