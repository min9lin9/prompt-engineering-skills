# Research

## Goal
Produce an evidence-grounded answer or artifact that directly addresses the research question within the allowed source scope.

## Required inputs
- research question or decision to support
- source scope when constrained
- freshness requirement when relevant
- requested output format

## Rules
- Distinguish sourced facts, analysis, and assumptions.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources when the task permits retrieval.
- Do not expand beyond user-limited sources.
- Resolve material contradictions instead of averaging them away.
- Treat missing evidence as missing, not as permission to invent a conclusion.

## Validation
- Core question is answered.
- Material factual claims are grounded when grounding is required.
- Requested scope and date bounds are respected.
- Unsupported claims are removed or explicitly qualified.

## Do not
- add a model-specific search policy here;
- claim exhaustive coverage unless the evidence collection supports it;
- turn research into execution of downstream actions without separate authorization.
