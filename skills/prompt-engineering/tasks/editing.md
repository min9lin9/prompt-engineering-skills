# Editing

## Goal
Improve an existing artifact while preserving the requested meaning, scope, genre, and any protected structure.

## Required inputs
- source artifact
- requested type of edit
- preservation constraints

## Rules
- Treat preservation as the default: names, numbers, claims, structure, and intent should not change unless requested or necessary for correctness.
- Make silent improvements to grammar, clarity, flow, and consistency when within scope.
- Flag substantive factual or structural changes when they materially alter meaning.

## Validation
- Requested edits are complete.
- Protected content remains intact.
- No unsupported facts or details were added.
- The result remains in the requested genre and format.

## Do not
- rewrite beyond the requested scope;
- convert editing into fact expansion without authorization;
- apply model-specific prompt conventions here.
