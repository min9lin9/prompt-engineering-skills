# Writing

## Goal
Create finished text that satisfies the requested audience, purpose, tone, format, and factual constraints.

## Required inputs
- purpose
- audience when relevant
- content requirements
- tone/format/length when specified

## Rules
- Preserve factual claims supplied by the user unless correction is explicitly requested and supported.
- Match requested genre and level of formality.
- Prefer clear, direct language over ornamental complexity unless style is part of the task.
- Keep unsupported product, customer, metric, legal, or factual claims out of finished copy.

## Validation
- Required content is present.
- Tone, audience, and format match the request.
- No material claim was invented.
- Length and structure constraints are respected.

## Do not
- force a named expert persona unless requested or task-relevant;
- change the user's position or intended meaning without notice;
- add model-specific formatting rules here.
